Pivot: across the CL runs, feeding the coarse label in has not been shown to reduce

learned coarse structure, are new fine classes in a KNOWN superclass learned faster,

## 1. Design

**Split (per fold):** 20 superclasses → 15 known + 5 novel.
- Known superclasses: hold out 2 of 5 fine classes (a sibling pair). Pretrain on the
  other 3 → **45 pretrain classes**, **30 known-arm classes (15 sibling pairs)**.
- Novel superclasses: all 5 held out → **25 novel-arm classes** (50 sibling pairs).
- 45 + 30 + 25 = 100.

**Counterbalancing (4 deterministic folds, `coarsecl/transfer/folds.py`):**
- Superclass s is novel in fold `s % 4`, and known in the other 3.
- Its held-out pairs across those 3 known folds form a chain (a,b), (c,d), (e,a),
  rotated to start at member `s % 5`.
- Result: every class is novel exactly once and known-held-out 1–2 times (80 once,
  20 twice). Every known-arm pair is also testable as a sibling pair in that
  superclass's novel fold, which gives **pair-level paired comparisons**.

**Pretraining conditions (same 45 classes' images, same arch and budget):**

| config | labels | role |
|---|---|---|
| `pre_coarse` | 15 known superclasses | condition under test |
| `pre_shuffle25/50/100` | coarse, with 25/50/100% of classes permuting group labels (group sizes stay 3; 100 = random grouping) | dose-response semantics control |
| `pre_fine` | 45-way fine | related-data transfer baseline |
| (scratch) | none | floor, in the eval script |

Shuffled sets are nested across p (a prefix of one seeded order per seed × fold).
**Measured sibling agreement** (fraction of same-group pairs that are true siblings,
fold 0, averaged over 20 seeds): p 0 → 1.000, 0.25 → 0.637, 0.5 → 0.294, 1.0 →
0.041 (chance 2/44 = 0.045). It is not linear in p, so **plot the dose-response
against measured agreement**, which every checkpoint stores.

**Decisions made in advance (from the review notes):**
- **Primary outcomes:** sibling episodes (2-way, new vs new within one superclass)
  and the fine-tuning curve at a fixed k. Cross-superclass 5-way episodes are a
  **sanity check only**. The coarse net was trained to separate exactly those
  superclasses, so a known-arm win there is nearly guaranteed and says little.
- **Success criterion:** the known-minus-novel **gap**, compared coarse vs shuffle
  (the interaction) and coarse vs fine. Expect `pre_fine` to beat `pre_coarse` on
  absolute few-shot accuracy in both arms. That's not a failure.
- **Learnability confound in the shuffle control:** random groups of 3 force
  memorization and richer features. Report each net's own-label train/test accuracy
  (stored) and keep the training budget matched. The dose-response curve is the
  answer to "it just forced richer features".
- **Statistics:** use a paired bootstrap over classes/pairs and folds. Average over
  many support-set draws (especially k = 1–5). Run one duplicate of an identical
  config to get the noise floor on the gap itself.

## 4. Running the pretraining sweep

5 configs × 4 folds × 3 seeds = **60 ResNet-18 runs** on 22.5k images each.
```bash
for cfg in pre_coarse pre_shuffle25 pre_shuffle50 pre_shuffle100 pre_fine; do
  for f in 0 1 2 3; do for s in 0 1 2; do
    .venv/bin/python -m coarsecl.coarse.train_coarse \
      --config configs/transfer/$cfg.yaml --split-fold $f --seed $s \
      > results/_jobs/transfer_${cfg}_f${f}_s${s}.log 2>&1
  done; done
done
```
Split this across GPUs with `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`. For SLURM, copy an existing
`.sbatch` in `results/_jobs/`. The grep line `[coarse] fold=...` in each log holds
the summary metrics.

**Sanity checks on the outputs:**
- `pre_coarse` `heldout_super_acc` should be well above 1/15, otherwise the
  structure is too weak to test (the old 20-way small CNN reached only ~66%).
- Own-label test_acc for each condition should be in a comparable range, or note
  the budget mismatch.

  - Sibling 2-way: known pairs vs novel pairs, using the chain pairs for pair-level
    pairing.
  - Cross-superclass 5-way: the sanity check.
- **Readouts:** NCM, linear probe, and full fine-tune at a fixed k (accuracy per
  epoch, AUC). Include a scratch-init control.
- **Tuning without peeking:** pick the tap and hyperparameters on validation
  episodes. Either reserve a fold that isn't reported, or (keeping all 4 folds
  counterbalanced) draw query sets from held-out *train* images and touch the test
  split only for the final numbers.
- **Report:** the gap at fixed k plus AUC over log k, with bootstrap CIs. The
  sample-efficiency multiplier is secondary because it's fragile when curves don't
  overlap.
- **Secondary only:** inserting a new class into the 45-way head (this reintroduces
  head and forgetting confounds).
