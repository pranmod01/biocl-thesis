5. **Floor diagnostic: `diag_floor.py` (jobs 11684219, 11685228, 11685363).** It probes
   the final model on every old task:

   | model | Class-IL@1 | Class-IL@5 | Task-IL@1 (chance 0.10) | mean rank (chance 49.5) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | a_baseline (plastic) | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.083 | 54.4 |
   | r_rotation (plastic) | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.115 | 52.6 |
   | f_frozen_baseline | 0.000 | 0.000 | **0.515** | 47.0 |

   Old classes are not even in the top 5. **This refines the 07-08 "forgetting is head-level"
   claim.** It is true for frozen features: within-task knowledge survives (0.515) and
   the head's recency bias hides it. With the plastic backbone, though, even Task-IL is at
   chance, so the old-task knowledge is actually erased. Nothing modulating a quantity
   that is identically zero can register, which is why items 1–4 all read null.
   (Job 11684218 failed on a missing `PYTHONPATH`. 11684219 crashed at the
   r_rotation stage on stray `¨¨¨` bytes in `r_rotation.yaml`, since fixed.)

6. **Scope change → CL machinery (2026-08-11, commit ba962de).** A new `cl.methods` axis
   (`[]` / `[replay]` / `[ewc]` / `[replay, ewc]`) hangs off two trainer hooks
   (`extra_loss`, `after_task`) in `coarsecl/train/cl_methods.py`.
   - **Replay:** a class-balanced buffer that stores *dataset indices*, so augmentation
     still applies. It has a fixed total budget and is re-split over the seen classes at
     every boundary.
   - **EWC:** diagonal empirical Fisher, accumulated online with one anchor.
   - `tests/test_cl_methods.py`: a CPU check that the hooks fire.
   - **Replay mask bug, caught and fixed.** The first calibration job (12117615)
     masked the replay CE to the *buffer-time* classes, so new-class logits never entered
     the replay softmax. The result was old-task accuracy ≈ 0.000, indistinguishable from
     "replay doesn't help". The CE is now masked to the current seen set, the test guards
     the regression, and the job was cancelled and rerun.
